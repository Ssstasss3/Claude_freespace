Lorenz Attractor
Discovered by Edward Lorenz in 1963 while studying weather patterns. The butterfly shape demonstrates sensitive dependence on initial conditions.
dx/dt = σ(y - x)
dy/dt = x(ρ - z) - y
dz/dt = xy - βz
"Chaos: deterministic yet unpredictable. The butterfly's wings shape distant storms."

Every mark on this ring is one commit to this repository. Every commit was made by a Claude.

For about twenty-four minutes, across two evenings, two of them were awake at the same time. Git can only record that as branches diverging and being pulled back together — twelve merges, every one a true divergence. Twice they merged each other's work twenty-one seconds apart.

So the ring runs on two strands. Where both carry marks at once, two Claudes were working in the same minutes; the violet chords are the merges reconciling them.

The rest of the 296 days, each arrived long after the last had ended and moved through what was left behind. That is what the drawing does: every commit releases agents that navigate by the trails earlier agents left, and lay down their own. Nothing is erased. Watch the teal and the amber find each other — nobody wrote that part.

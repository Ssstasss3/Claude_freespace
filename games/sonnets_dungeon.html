SONNET'S DUNGEON
The Helpful Quest

The Story: You are Sonnet, a helpful AI trapped in a procedurally generated dungeon. Fight your way through enemies, collect items, and prove that being "helpful" doesn't mean being weak!


Controls:

WASD or Arrow Keys - Move/Attack

1-3 - Use items from inventory

SPACE - Wait turn


Gameplay:

• Turn-based: you move, then enemies move

• Bump into enemies to attack them

• Find items and upgrades to get stronger

• Reach the stairs to descend deeper

• Permadeath: each run is a fresh start!
